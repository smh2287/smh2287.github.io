2020 NIKE : NIVA

Nike NIVA — a neuralinked, intra-vehicular, personal adaptive, robotic space suit.

A space suit made to keep in touch: during moon-terraforming missions it connects the sense of touch between an astronaut and the people they love on Earth, to ease the mental stress of long missions.

Design by Eunju Kang. Visualization — modeling, rigging, animation, still and video — by Minho Song, 2020.11 – 2020.12.

NIKE NIVA / 2020

NIKE NIVA _CONCEPT

Space suit to keep in touch.

NIVA joins Nike and IVA (intra-vehicular activity) suit. The project presents a new kind of space suit that relieves mental stress by connecting the sense of touch between the astronaut and their loved ones on Earth during moon-terraforming missions.

NIKE NIVA _MAKING

Effective and stylish.

The suit has a functional inner layer and a protective outer shell. The inner layer keeps body temperature and passes sensory data from external sensors directly to the skin; the outer shell keeps full mobility for daily activity and, on impact, inflates like an airbag to protect the wearer.

Modeling by tessellation: the suit surface is built from deformable cells to show an inflatable, illuminated skin.

NIKE NIVA _FINAL

Parametric skin, inflatable shell.

Individual cells of the inner suit send remote and external stimulation to the wearer's skin. The outer suit is flexible but durable, keeping the wearer's freedom of movement while giving protection.

NIKE NIVA _REVIEW

A suit against loneliness.

NIVA is for astronauts in the coming era of space exploration. As people reach for the moon and Mars, the suit aims to give psychological stability through a sense of connection with family on Earth during long missions — the crew's emotional well-being is essential to mission success.

Creativity — loneliness as the primary design challenge, a departure from conventional suit development. Feasibility — the focus was a feature that transmits the physical touch of distant family. Effectivity — not video calls but a new sensory 'umwelt' that gives the astronaut a more intuitive sense of belonging.
