2019–2022 VISHOW : CREATIVE VISUALIZATION

VISHOW — a creative visualization group.

It began as a project to visualize the creative works of designers around the world through new kinds of production, with the goal of becoming a digital space where creators gather, share their work and communicate.

Creative visualizer, 2019.12 – 2022.03. Most pieces start from another designer's original concept; the scene, story and image are the visualization.

VISHOW / 2019–2022

VISHOW _CONCEPT

Creative visualization group.

VISHOW began as a project to visualize the creative works of global designers through innovative production. Its ultimate goal is a digital space where diverse creators can gather to share their work and foster collaborative communication.

VISHOW _RENAULT XY-2

Romantic luxury trip.

The debut work. XY-2 is a mobility concept centred on the romance between two strangers; the imagery shows how an unforgettable journey in this vehicle could enrich and deepen their bond.

VISHOW _KTM F35

Underground runners.

The F35 is a next-generation motorcycle designed to maximise speed through an innovative layout and riding posture. The imagery imagines the dynamic experience of crossing urban landscapes at high speed.

VISHOW _AUDI EX MACHINA

Remarkable history.

Ex Machina is an autonomous mobility concept for premium luxury. To bring out its innovative nature it is placed in a historical setting — the moment early electric-vehicle pioneers witness the leap into the future.

VISHOW _BMW EXPERIENCE NOW

Lively enjoyment, mobility as a space.

Experience Now turns a vehicle into functional furniture that redefines how space is used. A surreal beach party of miniature figures shows the creative potential of the transformed interior; a second scene with human models shows its practical use and the meaning of the changed space.

VISHOW _MINI 3 WHEELER

Birth of new mobility.

Beyond simple 3D rendering, the aim was a distinct brand built on original IP: unconventional mobility concepts woven into their own narratives and characters, forming one immersive universe.

VISHOW _RENAULT ULTIMO

Unreal high-definition visuals.

The first stages were learned within the limits of Blender. A proficient pipeline then made room for more ambitious work, and Unreal Engine brought high-fidelity real-time visualization.

VISHOW _ALFA ROMEO STRADALE

Classic sleekness.

Methods were researched to capture both the realism and the classical beauty of automotive design in a live environment.

VISHOW _DESERT RUN

First cinematic project.

To go beyond the limits of still imagery, the group moved into video: Desert Run is a short cinematic sequence about the tension between two moving vehicles. It covered the whole production — storytelling, environment design and animation.

VISHOW _REVIEW

Mastering tools and creative cinematic direction.

A pivotal chance to build technical skill and learn cinematic direction for storytelling. The engagement of talented designers made high-fidelity results possible and gave valuable experience of collaborative production.

Creativity — continuous exploration of a visual language for each mobility design's identity. Feasibility — inexperienced members learned new skills together and set up a structured production pipeline. Effectivity — the pipeline and outputs built at VISHOW helped secure the industry–academy projects that followed.
