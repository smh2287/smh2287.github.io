VISHOW / 2019–2022

CONCEPT

Creative Visualization Group

vishow began as a project to visualize the creative works of global designers through innovative production. its ultimate goal is to evolve into a digital space where diverse creators can gather to share their work and foster collaborative communication.

RENAULT XY-2

Romantic Luxury Trip

our debut work, xy-2, is a mobility concept centered on the romance between two strangers. the imagery focuses on how an unforgettable journey facilitated by this vehicle can enrich and deepen their emotional bond.

KTM F35

AUDI EX MACHINA

Remarkable History

ex-machina is an autonomous mobility concept offering a premium luxury experience. to highlight its innovative nature, we envisioned the vehicle in a historical setting, capturing the moment early electric vehicle pioneers witness this leap into the future.

BMW EXPERIENCE NOW

Lively Enjoyment

the ‘experience now’ concept focuses on transforming a vehicle into functional furniture to redefine the usage of space. to illustrate this versatility, we envisioned a surreal beach party scene featuring miniature figures, highlighting the creative potential of the transformed environment.

MINI 3 WHEELER

RENAULT ULTIMO

Unreal High Definition Visuals

while initial stages involved learning and working within the constraints of blender, establishing a proficient pipeline allowed for more ambitious experimentation. we successfully implemented unreal engine to achieve high-fidelity, real-time visualization, significantly elevating the project’s visual quality.

ALFA ROMEO STRADALE

DESERT RUN

First Cinematic Project

we transitioned into video production to convey more dynamic stories. the “desert run” project is a short cinematic sequence capturing the tension between two moving vehicles. through this work, we mastered the end-to-end production process, including storytelling, environment design, and animation.

REVIEW

Mastering Tools and Creative Cinematic Direction

this project was a pivotal opportunity to enhance technical proficiency and master cinematic direction for impactful storytelling. the active engagement and contributions of talented designers were instrumental in achieving high-fidelity results, providing invaluable experience in high-level collaborative production.
