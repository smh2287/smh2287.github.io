2025 Mercedes-Benz : CLE CABRIOLET

AI-integrated, high-quality automotive visualization of the Mercedes-Benz CLE Cabriolet.

An open-top grand tourer that pairs an elegant design with a premium aesthetic. The work concentrates on the car's graceful silhouette and on high-fidelity detail, set in the lifestyle of the people it is made for.

Starting from an existing base model: optimization, rigging, animation, still and video.

MERCEDES BENZ / 2025

CLE CABRIOLET _BRAND STUDY

Modern open-air luxury.

Representing the pinnacle of emotional design, the CLE Cabriolet demands a precise balance of form and atmosphere for premium users.

Polar white car paint, carbon fibre, black leather, interior fabric, perforated aluminium.

CLE CABRIOLET _MODEL

High-quality model optimization.

Starting from the original model by hkv-studio, the UVs were re-edited and the car was rigged for sensitive posing and animation.

CLE CABRIOLET _EXTERIOR

Stance, elegance, openness.

A stance ready to dash forward expresses the cabriolet's dynamism. Delicate lighting brings out the elegance of the design in its fluid forms, and a wrap-around angle underscores the liberated feel of cruising with the top open.

CLE CABRIOLET _CLOSEUP

CLE CABRIOLET _INTERIOR

Premium detail, subtle ambience.

Textures of carbon, fine leather and stitching were meticulously rendered to express the interior craftsmanship. Soft ambient lighting delivers the car's gentle, sophisticated illumination.

CLE CABRIOLET _COLOUR VARIANT

Yellow study.

The same stills in a yellow exterior with a black interior. Kept on the working canvas; not part of the printed portfolio.

CLE CABRIOLET _SCENE

Automotive visualization for premium.

The stills were taken further into lifestyle scenes with AI-integrated editing: the car where its audience would use it.

Creativity — demonstrating high-fidelity visualization fundamentals at industry-grade rendering quality. Feasibility — form, materials and lighting were controlled to give a realistic impression of a physical car. Effectivity — a white variant accentuates the model's elegance.
