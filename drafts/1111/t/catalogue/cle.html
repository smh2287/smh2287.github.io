I · Brand

Modern open-air luxury

Representing the pinnacle of emotional design, the CLE Cabriolet demands a precise balance of form and atmosphere for premium users.

II · Model

High-quality model optimization

Starting from an existing base model, the UVs were re-edited and the car was rigged for sensitive posing and animation.

III · Exterior

Stance, elegance, openness

A stance ready to dash forward; delicate lighting on fluid forms; a wrap-around angle for the open-top profile.

IV · Interior

Premium detail, subtle ambience

Carbon, fine leather and stitching rendered to express the interior craftsmanship; soft ambient light for the car's gentle illumination.

V · Scene

Automotive visualization for premium

An open-top grand tourer in the setting of its audience's lifestyle — graceful silhouette, high-fidelity detail.
