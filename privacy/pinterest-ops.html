OpenClaw Pinterest Ops Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026

Overview

OpenClaw Pinterest Ops is a private automation tool used only by the owner to manage their own Pinterest account, boards, and Pins.

Information Accessed

After the owner grants OAuth permission, the app may access Pinterest account information, boards, Pins, Pin metadata, links, descriptions, and image URLs through the Pinterest API.

How Information Is Used

Pinterest data is used only for owner-requested workflows, including:

  • listing Pinterest boards;
  • listing Pins within boards;
  • organizing reference images and metadata;
  • exporting board inventories;
  • downloading images requested by the owner; and
  • optionally creating, updating, or deleting Pins requested by the owner.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine. The app does not operate a public user database and does not provide public user accounts.

Sharing

Pinterest data and OAuth tokens are not sold, shared, transferred, or disclosed to third parties.

Data Deletion

The owner can revoke the app's Pinterest access from their Pinterest account settings and can delete locally stored OAuth tokens from their own machine at any time.
